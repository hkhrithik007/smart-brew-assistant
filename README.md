☕ Smart Brew Assistant

A versatile brewing tool helper designed to help you create, manage, and perfect your daily coffee recipes. 

**Current Status:** For now, this tool is focused exclusively on the world-renowned **James Hoffmann brewing methods** (V60, French Press, and AeroPress). It provides precise temperature guides and step-by-step pouring timelines tailored to your exact coffee beans. 

**🚀 Upcoming AI Feature:** Please stay tuned! In the future, I will be integrating an AI-assisted recipe generator. This feature will act as your personal digital barista, helping you create entirely new, custom brew recipes on the fly to perfectly match your taste preferences and specific beans.

**🤝 Contribute:** You may also help me build this! Whether you want to add new brewing methods, improve the UI, or help lay the groundwork for the AI features, contributions and pull requests are highly welcome.

---

## 🌟 Current Features

* **Dynamic Temperature Matrix:** Automatically calculates the perfect water temperature (86°C - 96°C) by cross-referencing 6 different roast levels with 6 fermentation/processing types.
* **Real-Time Scaling:** Adjust your coffee dose (grams) or tweak the water ratio slider, and watch the target water volume and pouring timeline update instantly.
* **James Hoffmann Timelines:** 
  * **Pour Over (V60):** Calculates exact scale weights for the 5-pour technique.
  * **French Press:** Maps out the ultimate no-plunge steep, stir, and skim method.
  * **AeroPress:** Generates the inverted method timeline.
* **Modern UI:** Built with Tailwind CSS, featuring a responsive, glass-morphism aesthetic that looks great on both desktop and mobile devices.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **HTML5:** Semantic structure.
* **Tailwind CSS (via CDN):** For rapid, utility-first styling and responsive design.
* **Vanilla JavaScript:** Handles DOM manipulation, event listeners, and the multi-dimensional array logic for the temperature matrix without any heavy frameworks.
